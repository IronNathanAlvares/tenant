 Setting of rent above market rent prohibited. 
 19 
 19. —(1) In
 setting, at any particular time, the rent under the tenancy of a dwelling, an amount
 of rent shall not be provided for that is greater than the amount of the market rent
 for that tenancy at that time. 
 (2) The reference in this
 section to the setting of the rent under a tenancy is a reference to— 
 ( a ) the initial
 setting of the rent under the tenancy, and 
 ( b ) any subsequent
 setting of the rent under the tenancy by way of a review of that rent.
